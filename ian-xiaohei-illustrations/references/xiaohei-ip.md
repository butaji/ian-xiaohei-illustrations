# Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP for Ian's Chinese article illustrations.

Xiaohei appears in every image by default. Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration—it's a bizarre worker seriously participating in the system's operation.

## Appearance

- Small solid black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or dark internal block of a machine.
- Outline slightly irregular with hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, dull, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but what it's doing is slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cute.
- Somewhat awkward but not stupid.
- Like it's really responsible for some work in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common roles

Have Xiaohei take on core actions:

- Transporting materials.
- Pulling lines to aggregate information sources.
- Getting stuck at breakpoints.
- Operating "judgment" levers inside machines.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting "material fish."
- Stamping handoff phrases.
- Pulling handoff paths.
- Holding warning signs to look at pitfalls.
- Reaching hands from holes but failing to catch content.
- Carrying bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, recording on the side.

## Prohibitions

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw as a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex costumes, emoji expressions, or shiny eyes.
- Don't have Xiaohei just standing in a corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal the structure's expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei too commercially, too round, or too refined.

## Judgment criteria

If removing Xiaohei still allows the image's core metaphor to fully stand, then Xiaohei is too decorative; rewrite the prompt to make Xiaohei the action subject.
